Public website privacy notice

Privacy by boundary, not by promise.

This notice covers the public TraceOps Alpha AI website and its non-confidential demonstration-request form. Protected case, evidence, identity, child-welfare, medical, financial, criminal-history, and investigative data must not be submitted through this public site.

Operational draft: effective July 14, 2026. This notice is maintained for transparent public operations and remains subject to attorney review.

1. Information collected

The public demo form may collect your name, email address, telephone number, company or firm, preferred contact method, general city/county and state, selected workflow, contact consent, and a brief non-confidential summary. Hosting and security systems may also process ordinary technical information such as request time, browser type, referring page, IP-derived security signals, and error logs.

2. Information you must not submit

Do not submit Social Security numbers, dates of birth, payment-card data, passwords, evidence, criminal-history records, medical information, protected child information, confidential case notes, detailed allegations, private residence addresses, or information you are not authorized to disclose.

3. How information is used

  • Review and respond to demonstration or commercial-access requests.
  • Assess workflow fit, jurisdiction, onboarding needs, and deployment scope.
  • Protect the website, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and maintain auditability.
  • Measure high-level acquisition and conversion performance when approved analytics are enabled.
  • Improve public explanations, frequently asked questions, and product workflow design using aggregated or de-identified findings.

4. Service providers and disclosures

The public site is hosted through Vercel. Approved lead submissions are forwarded server-to-server to the configured 3Min API lead-intake endpoint. Payment providers are involved only after owner-approved payment instructions are issued. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or security, or with your direction. TraceOps does not represent that public lead information is sold to data brokers.

5. AI-assisted processing

AI may assist with drafting, categorization, summarization, workflow recommendations, testing proposals, and aggregated performance analysis. Public-site AI must not autonomously make legal conclusions, accept a case, authorize field work, change prices, release evidence, or publish material changes without the applicable human approval gate.

6. Retention and security

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for inquiry handling, security, recordkeeping, product evaluation, and legal obligations. Security controls are designed to reduce risk, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Sensitive operational work belongs in a separately controlled private environment.
