Unified operations

Not another low-end case tracker.

TraceOps is designed as an operational control plane: it preserves source boundaries, exposes uncertainty, keeps sensitive work out of the public marketing layer, and makes every next action accountable.

01

Case command

Qualified leads, matter status, assignments, deadlines, supervisory review, and next-action discipline across the full case lifecycle.

02

Evidence vault

Source tagging, checksums, access history, retention controls, chain-of-custody context, and controlled export workflows.

03

Intelligence graph

People, entities, addresses, events, and records connected with confidence, provenance, and unresolved-association warnings.

04

Field operations

Authorized assignment planning, investigator check-ins, attempt logs, evidence capture, and safety-aware mobile workflows.

05

Human-reviewed AI

Chronology extraction, inconsistency detection, question generation, and drafting support without converting inference into fact.

06

Owner command

Founder authority, role boundaries, emergency access, billing control, infrastructure oversight, and immutable audit review.
